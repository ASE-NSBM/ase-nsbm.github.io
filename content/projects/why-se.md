# Why SE?

A flagship awareness session exploring the value and opportunities of pursuing a BSc in Software Engineering, with insights from industry experts and alumni. The event includes both an online session covering job roles and exposure, and a physical session on how to practice and master your LinkedIn profile and land jobs with GitHub.
