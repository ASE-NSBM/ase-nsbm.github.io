# Tech Carnival 25’

Tech Carnival 2025 was organized with a clear vision: to create an exciting, interactive, and educational space that welcomes new students into the dynamic world of the Faculty of Computing while strengthening the community spirit of the Association of Software Engineering (ASE).

Driven by our motive to blend fun with technology, the event featured a variety of innovative game stalls, puzzles, arcade challenges, and creative experiences designed to spark curiosity and inspire engagement. Beyond entertainment, the carnival served as a meaningful initiative to raise essential funds for upcoming ASE technical projects, including our CTF event and CSR ventures.
