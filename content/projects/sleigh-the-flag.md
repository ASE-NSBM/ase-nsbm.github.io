# Sleigh the Flag Inter-University CTF

Sleigh the Flag Inter University Christmas CTF was built as a vibrant, competitive, and practical learning arena where the brightest young cybersecurity minds of Sri Lanka could test their skills in a never-seen-before Christmas-themed CTF experience crafted entirely by university talent and Legion Offensive Security.

What began as a vision to elevate hands-on cybersecurity culture became a phenomenal reality, with the event successfully bringing together over 24 teams from across the nation from state universities such as Vavuniya, Jaffna, Ruhuna, and Sri Jayawardenapura, to leading private institutions including SLIIT, APIIT, ECU, ESU, and many more. The massive participation, the long-distance commitment, and the energy of every team reaffirmed why we built this event to unite the country’s top tech minds, ignite collaboration and competition, and take Sri Lanka’s cybersecurity spirit to the next level.
