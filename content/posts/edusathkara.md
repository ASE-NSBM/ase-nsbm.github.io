# EduSathkara - Crowdsourced Educational Resources Platform

Edu Sathkara is a nonprofit, technology-driven educational initiative launched to support Sri Lankan students whose learning was disrupted by Cyclone Ditwah. Designed as a centralized, crowdsourced resource bank, the platform enables students, teachers, and volunteers to share and access essential learning materials such as past papers, notes, textbooks, worksheets, and marking schemes across all grades, subjects, and all three mediums. Built using modern web technologies and a scalable cloud-based architecture, Edu Sathkara ensures easy access, structured categorization, and reliability even during times of crisis. Developed and maintained by the Association of Software Engineering at NSBM Green University, the platform represents a collective effort to use technology as a force for resilience, equity, and continuity in education.
