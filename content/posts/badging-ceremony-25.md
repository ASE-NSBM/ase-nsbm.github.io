# Subcommittee Badging Ceremony 2025

A formal recognition event organized to officially badge the newly appointed ASE subcommittee members, symbolizing their commitment and responsibilities for the upcoming term. The ceremony celebrated leadership, teamwork, and dedication, motivating new members to contribute toward the association’s goals while maintaining the prestige and unity of the ASE.
